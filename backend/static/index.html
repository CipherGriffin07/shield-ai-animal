The pipeline

From sighting to rescue, in three steps.

01

Report

Snap a photo, drop a pin, add a quick description. No account required for emergencies.

→
02

AI triage

Gemini Vision reads the photo and description to estimate injury, severity, and first aid — in seconds.

→
03

Rescue

The nearest available volunteer is matched automatically and the case is tracked to recovery.

Platform

Everything a rescue network needs, in one place.

Real-time tracking

A live timeline from "Report submitted" through "Recovered" — visible to reporters and rescuers alike.

Lost & found matching

Upload a photo of a found animal — SHIELD compares it against open lost reports nearby automatically.

SHIELD assistant

A rescue-trained chat assistant for first aid, emergency steps, and adoption or lost-pet guidance.

Auto-prioritization

Every case gets a 0–100 priority score from severity, mobility, and risk factors — critical cases surface first.

Smart matching

Volunteers are ranked by distance, vehicle access, species experience, and track record — best fit, first.

Compatibility-checked adoption

A short questionnaire scores how well a home fits an animal's temperament before applications go to NGOs.
